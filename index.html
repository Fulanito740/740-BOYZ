The Story

740 BOYZ came out of New York’s 90s dance and hip-house wave, fusing Dominican-American flavor with club-ready hooks and chant-along energy. The group’s breakout single, “Shimmy Shake,” became the calling card: direct, rhythmic, and instantly built for the room.

The legacy is simple and strong: one phrase, one beat, one record that still connects DJs, retro dance fans, and festival crowds who remember when the floor moved as one.
